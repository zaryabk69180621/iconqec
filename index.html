About the Quality Enhancement Cell (QEC)

The Quality Enhancement Cell (QEC) at Imperial College of Nursing, Kabal Swat serves as the central focal point for upholding academic rigor, clinical simulation integrity, and continuous institutional growth.

Established in accordance with the regulatory criteria of the Higher Education Commission (HEC) Pakistan and the Quality Assurance Agency (QAA) of Khyber Medical University (KMU), Peshawar, QEC bridges academic theory with practical nursing excellence. We systematically assess learning outcomes, monitor clinical rotations across affiliated teaching hospitals, and ensure transparent student and faculty feedback.

"Quality is not an isolated milestone; it is an enduring commitment to student empowerment, clinical patient safety, and compassionate nursing care."

— Directorate of Quality Assurance, Imperial College of Nursing Swat

Our Vision

To establish Imperial College of Nursing as a premier, nationally recognized center of nursing education distinguished by world-class clinical competence, ethical practice, and quality healthcare leadership in Swat Valley and beyond.

Our Mission

To institutionalize a systematic culture of quality assurance through comprehensive self-assessment, feedback elicitation, clinical station calibration, faculty development, and strict adherence to KMU, PN&MC, and HEC benchmarks.

Core Functions of QEC Office

A multi-faceted approach to guaranteeing academic, simulation, and clinical excellence in all nursing cohorts.

Self-Assessment Process (SAP)

Conducting in-depth program reviews and synthesizing standardized Self-Assessment Reports (SAR) to monitor syllabus completion, examination transparency, and learning outcomes.

Clinical & Skills Lab Audits

Regular verification of anatomical mannequins, clinical consumables, OSCE stations, hospital ward rotations, and preceptor supervision standards across affiliated hospitals.

Digital Feedback Elicitation

Collecting confidential, structured evaluations from students regarding classroom instruction, laboratory practicals, and clinical preceptors to drive institutional corrective actions.

Faculty Development & Training

Organizing workshops on modern Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE), Objective Structured Practical Examination (OSPE), and innovative medical pedagogies.

Course File Compliance

Verifying semester course files, lecture schedules, recommended nursing literature, and continuous formative assessment metrics for each semester cohort.

Institutional Portal & Transparency

Publishing official quality notifications, action plans, targets, and downloadable SAR documents on qec.iconswat.com for stakeholders and regulatory reviewers.

Student & Stakeholder Voice

Feedback Elicitation Portals

Your candid feedback directly drives the Quality Enhancement Cell's academic corrective measures, hospital rotations, and lab facilities. All responses are handled with strict institutional confidentiality.

Clinical & Skills Lab Training Evaluation

Assessing simulation mannequins, demonstration clarity, hospital rotations, and safety protocols.

100% Confidential Evaluation
Skills Lab Evaluation Parameters (Likert Scale: 1 = Poor to 5 = Excellent)
1. Availability and functional condition of anatomical models & simulation mannequins
2. Clarity and comprehensiveness of instructor demonstrations before hands-on practice
3. Adequate time allocated for each student to practice essential nursing procedures
4. Provision of consumables (syringes, cannulas, gloves, antiseptics) during practicals
5. Lab hygiene, lighting, ventilation, and adherence to safety & infection protocols
Clinical Hospital Rotation Parameters (Likert Scale: 1 = Poor to 5 = Excellent)
1. Quality and accessibility of clinical preceptor mentorship and bedside guidance
2. Exposure to diverse patient clinical cases aligned with course learning objectives
3. Timely verification and signature of student clinical logbooks and procedure records
4. Supportive and safe learning environment within the hospital wards and units
QEC Assurance: Your response is encrypted. Data is compiled in aggregated statistical format for the bi-annual Clinical Audit Report submitted to Khyber Medical University (KMU).

Course & Teacher Evaluation (HEC Proforma-1)

Standardized evaluation of curriculum delivery, instructor punctuality, and concept clarity.

HEC QAA Standard Format
Evaluation Criteria (1 = Strongly Disagree to 5 = Strongly Agree)
1. The teacher is punctual and regular in conducting scheduled lectures and discussions
2. The teacher comes well prepared and communicates course concepts with utmost clarity
3. The syllabus outline prescribed by KMU / PNC was covered comprehensively during the term
4. The teacher encourages student participation, critical questioning, and classroom discussions
5. Effective utilization of multimedia, audio-visual aids, and anatomical illustrations
6. Internal assessment (quizzes, assignments, mid-terms) is fair, transparent, and returned with feedback

The SAR is a comprehensive institutional document mandated by HEC and KMU. It critically reviews nursing program outcomes, curriculum coverage, laboratory infrastructure, and faculty competencies to identify areas for continuous enhancement.
